About Us

The IoT Club at the School of Instrumentation (SOI), DAVV is a community of innovators, thinkers, and makers. We provide a collaborative platform for students to move past theory and get their hands dirty with real-world technology.

Whether you're a beginner curious about sensors or an expert in cloud deployments, there's a place for you here.

Empowering Students

We believe in peer-to-peer learning, mentorship, and building things that matter. Join us in our journey of continuous learning and networking.

Our Wings

We operate in specialized wings, working together to build complete end-to-end IoT systems.

Hardware Wing

Focuses on the physical layer of IoT. Hands-on experience with PCB Design, Sensors, and Microcontrollers like ESP32 and Arduino.

Software Wing

Handles data processing, cloud storage, user interfaces, and machine learning models to bring hardware data to life.

Hackathons & Competitions

We actively form teams, provide mentorship, and participate in national-level events like the Smart India Hackathon.

Code of Conduct

All individuals associated with the IoT Club SOI must embody the following principles:

01
Respect: Treat everyone with dignity.
02
Integrity: Be honest in your work and interactions.
03
Accountability: Own your tasks, mistakes, and successes.
04
Teamwork: Support and uplift your peers.
05
Inclusivity: Welcome diverse perspectives and backgrounds.
06
Professionalism: Maintain high standards in communication and conduct.
07
Learning Mindset: Stay curious and open to feedback.
08
Safety: Prioritize physical and digital safety in all activities.
09
Creativity: Encourage innovative problem-solving.
10
Responsibility: Care for club resources and reputation.
11
Transparency: Communicate openly and clearly.
12
Discipline: Respect time, deadlines, and guidelines.
